# 🚗 Car Insurance Risk & Claim Analysis – Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Car Insurance Risk and Claim Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of insurance policies, claim amounts, customer demographics, vehicle information, and risk-related patterns. It allows users to interactively explore the data using filters and dynamic measures.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

![Instamart Sales Dashboard](https://github.com/Adityakaushik-mrt/Power-BI-Dashboard/blob/main/Insurance%20Risk%20&%20Claim%20Analysis/Insurance%20Dashboard.PNG?raw=true)

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze car insurance data and identify important patterns related to:

- Insurance policies
- Claim amounts
- Claim frequency
- Customer demographics
- Car usage and car makes
- Coverage zones
- Age groups
- Education and marital status
- Kids driving behavior
- Policy trends by car year

The dashboard can help insurance businesses understand customer segments, identify risk patterns, and support data-driven decision-making.

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Policies by Car Usage
Analyzes insurance policies based on:

- Private
- Commercial

### 2. Policies by Car Make
Identifies the most commonly insured car brands, including:

- Ford
- Chevrolet
- Dodge
- Toyota
- GMC
- Mitsubishi
- Pontiac
- Mazda

### 3. Policies by Coverage Zone
Analyzes policies across different geographic coverage zones:

- Urban
- Rural
- Highly Urban
- Suburban
- Highly Rural

### 4. Policies by Age Group
Provides an age-wise analysis of insurance policies across different customer age groups.

### 5. Policies by Car Year
Shows the trend of insurance policies based on the manufacturing year of vehicles.

### 6. Policies by Kids Driving
Analyzes policies based on the number of children/young drivers associated with the policy.

### 7. Policies by Education
Provides customer policy distribution based on educational background.

### 8. Claim Amount by Education & Marital Status
Provides a detailed comparison of claim amounts across:

- Education
- Marital Status

This helps identify customer segments associated with higher claim amounts.
